# Support

EchoWave is a community-maintained open-source project. Support is best effort; there is no service-level agreement or guaranteed response time.

## Where to ask

- **A reproducible app bug:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/AyushDevHub/EchoWave/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **A product idea:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/AyushDevHub/EchoWave/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security vulnerability:** follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post details publicly.
- **Build or setup help:** read [developer setup](docs/SETUP.md) and search existing issues first.

## Include useful information

Include the EchoWave version, device model, Android version, what you expected, what happened, and concise reproduction steps. Attach screenshots only when they help explain the issue.

Before posting, remove names, account information, cookies, request headers, PO tokens, full media URLs, signed URLs, and other private data from logs or screenshots. Do not upload music files or copyrighted media as a reproduction case.

EchoWave relies on third-party services that may change or restrict access independently. Service outages, catalog availability, stream resolution, and provider-side restrictions may be outside the project's control. Read the [provider notes](docs/INNER_TUBE_NOTES.md).
