# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

EchoWave welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to make project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Accept good-faith feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep reports, reviews, and project discussions relevant and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruptive conduct, personal attacks, or deliberate intimidation.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional project discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to EchoWave repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project spaces operated by the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, block participation, or report serious abuse to the relevant platform. Responses will be proportionate to the behavior and may be made without a public explanation where privacy requires it.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer through the GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
