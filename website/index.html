Good to know

An early, independent release

EchoWave reads from unofficial YouTube / YouTube Music interfaces, which may change or stop working without notice, and has no documented authorization from the provider. It does not host music and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube. Requires Android 8.0+. Release notes include a SHA-256 checksum so you can verify your download.

Questions

Is EchoWave free?

Yes. It is open source under GPL-3.0, with no subscription and no ads.

Where does the music come from?

EchoWave does not host any music. It resolves search, metadata and streams through third-party YouTube / YouTube Music endpoints.

Can I contribute?

Yes. Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub.
